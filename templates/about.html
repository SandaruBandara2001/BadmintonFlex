Revolutionizing Badminton Training with Real-Time Feedback

Who We Are

Badminton Flex was created to bridge the gap between beginner players and professional-level coaching.

Our Mission

To democratize access to professional badminton coaching through cutting-edge technology. Badminton Flex empowers athletes of all levels to improve their skills, track their performance, and receive real-time feedback—anytime, anywhere.

Our Technology

Badminton Flex combines biomechanics, computer vision pose analysis to deliver personalized coaching and instant feedback.

Pose Estimation

Real-time body tracking to detect posture and movement patterns, ensuring perfect form during play.

Machine Learning

Our shot classification model leverages CNNs to recognize different badminton strokes with high accuracy.

Instant Feedback

Receive corrective feedback immediately after each shot, improving technique and reducing errors quickly.
